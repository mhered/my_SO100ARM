# My notes on the 1st lerobot worldwide hackaton

14-15 june

52 hours 

over +3000 participants, 56% gathered in 100 local venues in 6 continents and the rest remotely

check out details on this document: [lerobot_hackaton_5_tracks.pdf](./assets/lerobot_hackaton_5_tracks.pdf)

hugging face company that provides largest platform for open source ai

community driven hub to share AI

seing progress in embodied AI / breakthrough in robotics 

lerobot initiative launched in feb 2024 to lower barriers to entry

lerobot is a full stack project combines affordable robot designs, software tools to record data sets, train AI models and run them on robots

+9000 on discord

+8000 datasets shared on huggingface hub!

largest open dataset for robotics

20M hours of recording

2 weeks ago they released rthe first vision-language-action model SmolVLA

 trained on this data that runs on a laptop



5 tracks:

Track #1 - Imitation learning. Needs robot and GPU access. Easiest. Tutorial to assemble setup, calibrat e and teleop with keyboard or leader arm.   Then train using simple models like ACT or more advanced like smolVLA. To go further: async inference online corrections or data augmentation


Track #2 - Reinforcement learning. Needs local GPU, main arm and device for teleop. They have a dedicated tutorial, but I didnt understand the intro. Can be followed also in simulation using gym-HIL 



Track #3 - add your own robot to lerobot. only need a robot. Dedicaterd tutorial! Also design a teleoperator

Track #4 - Suggest hardware improvements: new gripper. new design, new sensors, audio sensors

Track #5 - dataset tools improvements: e.g. streamline data creation, or edition, improve user experience, add new data types to the format sensors etc



3 rules

1 demo video/team under 1' by 6PM local on 15/6

30 winners

* creativity
* technical difficulty
* usability
* real-world impact
